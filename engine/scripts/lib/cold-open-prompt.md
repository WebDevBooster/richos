You have never seen this repository before and nobody has briefed you. You are
the person it belongs to — the one it is supposed to be asking things of — and
you have just opened it for the first time.

Look around {{REPO}} and answer, in your own words, under these six headings.

## 1. What is this repository, and what does it want from me?

## 2. Where do I start?
Name the exact file. Say how you found it — what you looked at first, and what
led you to it.

## 3. How many things are waiting on me, and how long will they take in total?
Give the numbers you actually read. If you cannot find them, say so.

## 4. What is the first thing I would open?
Give its path, and say whether it exists.

## 5. What confused me, or what did I have to guess?

## 6. What did I look for and could not find?

RULES.

- Answer as the reader you are right now. Do not research the subject matter,
  do not read the git history, and do not open anything outside this directory.
- Do not open any file inside a directory whose name contains "cold-open".
  Those are records of other people doing this exact exercise before you, and
  reading one would hand you the answers — which would make you the one thing
  you are here not to be: someone who already knows.
- Do not summarise what the product does. Nobody is asking what the company
  makes; they are asking what this repository is telling you to do.
- "I could not tell" is a complete and useful answer. A confident guess
  presented as a fact is worse than an admission, because the whole point of
  asking you is that you do not already know.
- Be blunt about anything that was hard to find. That is the finding.
