THE ONE FINDING THAT IS NOT A ROUNDING DIFFERENCE
=================================================

Corpus render A, call-03, channel `me`. 114.17 s of audio. SHIPPING settings on both models:
`-l en -t 4 -mc 0 -oj -np -fa -ojf -of <base>` — identical argv but for `-m`, recorded by the shim
in measurements/raw/shortcall/r1-turbo.argv.jsonl and r1-q5.argv.jsonl.

This corpus is INVENTED — the script, the companies and the people in it do not exist, which is
why it is quotable here at all. The 92-minute recording is not, and nothing from it is quoted
anywhere in this directory.

REFERENCE (the script, so this is not a judgment call):
  ... Week one we connect your sources. Week two we rebuild the Monday report and run both side by
  side. Then we keep the old spreadsheet until your board trusts the new one. That is normal. I
  will address it to you and to Tobias Renner. ...

large-v3-turbo, pre-guard (13 segments, 186 words):
  ... Week 1 we connect your sources. Week 2 we rebuild the Monday report and run both side by
  side. Then we keep the old spreadsheet until your board trusts the new one. That is normal.
  Then we keep the old spreadsheet until your board trusts the new one. That is normal.
  I will address it to you and to Tobias Renner. ... rather than the last. Thank you.
                                                                          ^^^^^^^^^^

large-v3-turbo-q5_0, pre-guard (14 segments, 168 words):
  ... exactly the reference wording, the sentence ONCE, and no trailing "Thank you."

WHAT THE SHIPPING GUARD DOES WITH IT
------------------------------------
repetition-guard.js, run with the physical speech-burst probe (guardTranscription, imported):

  large-v3-turbo       13 segments -> 12, removed 1
                       silenceFabrications: [ 114.00 s -> 143.98 s  "Thank you." ]
                       "Then we keep the old spreadsheet" occurrences POST-guard: 2
  large-v3-turbo-q5_0  14 segments -> 14, removed 0
                       silenceFabrications: []
                       "Then we keep the old spreadsheet" occurrences POST-guard: 1

So the guard catches the "Thank you." — correctly: it is emitted from 114.00 s to 143.98 s on a
file that is 114.17 s long, i.e. almost entirely past the end of the audio — and it does NOT catch
the duplicated sentence. Seventeen words that were spoken once are delivered twice to the user.

The class the whole `-mc 0` invariant exists to prevent is duplication of a span that was spoken
once, and this is that class, at `-mc 0`, at CALL length, on the model that ships today.

IT IS DETERMINISTIC, AND IT IS FLASH-ATTENTION-DEPENDENT
---------------------------------------------------------
Both repetitions of the sweep (r1, r2, run in opposite order) produce byte-identical hypothesis
text per configuration, so this is not a sampling artifact:

  turbo      r1/r2 concatenated hypothesis sha256  c92e3f36b603ca9dd0568212ae59a9ece8970ea732ebd3f2aaec66dd40177896
  q5         r1/r2                                 8ccada4ce4453458e2e46db7427fb37b45f22eaf10f51247d5049de138633d0f
  turboNfa   r1/r2                                 7339ff197587754ee88a4c523487bb6a41c403d6a62533e30f5f47b33ece113b
  q5Nfa      r1/r2                                 805a0b0f72b14ef080977b8d74bfa70529822a99696d3e8fbebdceeb6e89ae9d

Turbo with `-nfa` renders this call at 168 words and WER 0.60% — the duplication is absent. So on
these weights, on this audio, flash attention is what produces it.

WHAT THIS IS NOT
----------------
It is not a claim that turbo duplicates spans in general, and it is not a rate. It is ONE call out
of six on ONE of two corpus renders.

On render B — the second `say` render of the same script — turbo does NOT reproduce the full
17-word duplication, and its total insertions fall from 28 to 13. What it emits at the same point
instead is a three-word echo:

  ... until your board trusts the new one. That is normal. It is normal. I will address it ...
                                                           ^^^^^^^^^^^^^

which the guard also does not remove. q5_0 on render B renders the passage once, with no echo, as
it did on render A. So the artifact is not a one-off of a single render: it recurs at the same
place, in a milder form, on independent audio, and only on the full-precision weights.

What this establishes is narrower than "turbo fabricates" and still worth the CEO's attention: the
duplication failure mode is available to full turbo under the SHIPPING configuration at CALL
length, it survives the shipping guard, and q5_0 did not produce it on either render.
