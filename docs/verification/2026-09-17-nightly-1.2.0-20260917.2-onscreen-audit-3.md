### What my harness could and could not do with the microphone

**This is a harness limitation, not an app defect, and it is recorded as one.** I have no voice. The
only way I can put sound into a microphone on this machine is to play machine speech (`say`) out of
the **Mac mini Speakers** and let the **Elgato Wave:3** on his desk hear it across the room. That is
a degraded acoustic path — even pacing, room reflections, speaker colouration, unknown distance —
and it is not how a person uses this.

- **Could:** at output volume 85, one such utterance was captured, transcribed, sent, answered, and
  the answer **spoken back aloud**. The whole voice pipeline ran end to end from it.
- **Could not:** at volume 60 the same sentence registered **nothing**. A 2.3-second utterance came
  back from the recogniser as `(clears throat)` and was dropped.
- **What would have made it possible:** injecting a WAV into a **virtual input device**. That needs
  installing one (BlackHole or similar), and this brief forbids installing anything — so I did not.
  The gap was closed instead by the CEO speaking into the real microphone himself.

**So no negative observation about speech recognition quality in this audit is charged to the app.**
Defect #1 is charged to the app because it is read from the app's own log and reproduced with **his**
voice, not mine.

## 4. The new defects, with repro and control

**#1 — you cannot talk over Rich.** *Basis: read from the running app's log; reproduced with two
different speakers.* Boot announces `aec=PBFDAF 2048 taps (128 ms tail)` and `barge-in=313 frames
(5.008 s)`. Runtime logs, twice, `discarded audio captured while Rich was speaking (no AEC)`. The
timing is exact: the CEO's second prompt was received at **13:34:02Z**, the very second the previous
turn completed — so the part of his sentence spoken over Rich's answer was discarded and only the
tail, `"using talk to…"`, survived to be sent. Rich answered the tail and had to ask what he meant.
**Expected:** either barge-in works as the boot line advertises, or the app does not advertise it and
tells the person their microphone is off while Rich talks. **Control:** the same microphone, the same
session, with Rich silent, captured `"This is Alex."` complete and correctly punctuated. **What would
settle the remaining question** — whether the cut is only the discard window or also an
end-of-utterance cut — is one person speaking one uninterrupted sentence longer than four seconds
while Rich is silent.

**#2 — home screen clipped at 1024x700.** *Basis: ran it on screen.* Unchanged from audit-2 #4 and
re-confirmed on a fresh launch at `a3-22`. No new information; it is in this list because it is what
he sees first.

**#3 — Return does not send, and nothing says what does.** *Basis: ran it on screen, with the
keystroke's arrival proven.* Type into the message box and press Return: nothing sends. Pressed
twice, with the accessibility tree confirming the text area focused (`AXTextArea … AXFocused true`)
both times. **The keystroke is not being lost** — reading the box's value back byte for byte
afterwards gives `Reply with exactly: hello from the nightly.\n`, so Return arrived and was inserted
as a newline. `Cmd`+`Return` sends. The **▷** control sends. **Control:** both alternatives worked
first time in this same session. **Expected:** in a message box, Return sends and Shift+Return makes
a newline — or, if this app means the opposite, the box says so. Nothing on screen mentions either
gesture. *(Whether the newline is by design is a design question, not a QA verdict — flagged, not
decided.)*

**#4 — a dropped transcript is silent.** *Basis: ran it on screen, with the log as the counterpart.*
Spoke a 2.3-second sentence; the recogniser returned `(clears throat)`; the app logged
`discarded non-speech transcript: "(clears throat)"` and **the window did not change at all**
(`a3-15`). Dropping it is the right call. Saying nothing is not: the person is left unable to tell
the difference between "not heard", "heard and discarded" and "broken". **Expected:** a one-line,
non-blocking "I didn't catch that."

**#5 — the newest card sits below the fold.** *Basis: ran it on screen.* After the corpus sheet was
deferred, the thread stayed scrolled where it was, with a "↓" chip over the last card (`a3-02`,
`a3-03`). Minor, but it is the moment a person is looking for what just happened.

**Watched, not ranked.** One click on the settings control produced no panel (`a3-18`); the next one
did. **I am not calling that a defect**, because I cannot tell from one frame whether the click
missed the target, was eaten by a tooltip that was up at the time, or toggled the panel open and shut
— and a single unreproduced frame is not a bug report. It is here so that whoever sees it twice knows
it has been seen once.
