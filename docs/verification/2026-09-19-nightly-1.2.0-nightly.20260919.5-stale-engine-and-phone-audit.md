## Question 1 — the refresh offer on entry

### The offer is on screen, and that is the real gain

Frame `q1-offer-on-screen-at-entry.png` is the app as Rich left it, untouched, six minutes after launch. The sheet **is there**, in front, over the restored thread, with `Set it up` and `Not now`. That is the .15 blocker's substance fixed: nobody has to press the door first.

### It does not hold focus, and this is the part that failed

    to get value of attribute "AXFocusedUIElement"'

### R3, phone → Mac: 7,288 ms, and the message and its reply land in one paint

**This is the method in full, because it is the number that decides the question.**

- The phone's composer was filled with `phone-to-mac-candidate-sixteen` and left focused. Nothing else was pending.
- A Mac-side capture loop was started over the window region at ~45 ms per frame. `t0` is the instant the `adb shell input tap 608 895` call was **issued** from the Mac; the call returned 109 ms later, so the true tap landed somewhere inside that 109 ms and every number below is **generous to the app** by up to 109 ms.
- Both devices were on the same thread (`Running`), the Mac with that thread open and visible.
- Frames were then diffed two ways: whole-window, and restricted to the thread pane alone (x ≥ 430), because the sidebar's activity dot and the composer's blinking caret otherwise dominate every diff.

What the two diffs say:

| Region | First change |
|---|---|
| whole window | **+471 ms** — a small activity dot appears on the `Running` row in the sidebar (bbox (247,265)–(417,656)) |
| thread pane only (x ≥ 430) | **+7,288 ms** (bbox (0,4)–(555,461) within the pane, area 253,635) |

So the Mac *knew* within about half a second and said so with a dot the size of a full stop. The conversation itself stayed unchanged for **7.3 seconds**.

**How I know the message and the reply landed in the same paint.** The two consecutive frames either side of the transition are in the evidence folder:

## Question 6 — §62, the curtain

**A correction first, because it changes what "the curtain" means.** The opening curtain is not the `for you` home screen. It is a separate, transient surface (`richos/app/ui/splash.js`) — the RichOS mark, "The Operating System for the AI-Enabled CEO", and a progress bar — and it yields on the first of three signals: the app reporting ready, the first keystroke, or a hard ceiling. My first attempt tested the home screen four minutes after launch, where space simply enters the thread. That is a `home.js` result and it is **not** a §62 result; it is not reported as one.

The measurement was made with a window-keyed trap: watch for a new `richos-tauri` process whose parent is launchd, then wait for its **window** to exist before pressing anything. The first version of the trap waited only for the process, and its keystroke went to whatever was frontmost because the window had not been created yet — that is why the corrected trap exists.
