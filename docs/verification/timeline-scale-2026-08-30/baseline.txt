WHAT THE NUMBERS SAY
--------------------
The documents named the wrong defect. At the promised 10,000 items:

  * SCROLLING WAS ALREADY FINE — 17ms p50, which is this engine's vsync floor. Nothing
    needed virtualizing to make the CEO's scroll smooth.
  * THE LIVE PATH WAS NOT. A structural change cost 257ms, and a structural change is
    every activity row, every worker upsert, every turn-status transition. A 52-second
    multi-tool turn emits dozens. That is a fifteen-frame stall, once per tool call, for
    the whole of a long turn — and it is invisible in any test that renders once.
  * THE ALL-EXPANDED CASE SCROLLED AT ~26ms p50 (~40fps), 51,001 nodes. Below smooth, and
    reachable by the CEO opening work transcripts.
