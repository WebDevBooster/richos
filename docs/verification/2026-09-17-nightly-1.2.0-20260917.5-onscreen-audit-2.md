## Verdict

**NOT READY.**

Your blocker from candidate .4 is genuinely fixed — and the same audio path now does something
worse than the thing it was fixed for.

**On speakers, Rich's own spoken answer was transcribed and sent to the model as the CEO's
message.** At 20:06:43.693Z, three seconds after the model turn had ended and while Rich was still
speaking his answer aloud, the ledger recorded:

```
PromptReceived  source=jam  text="1, 2, 3, 4, 5."
```

That is Rich's own counting — *"One... two... three... four... five..."* — heard back through the
Elgato Wave:3, recognized as speech, submitted as though the CEO had said it, and answered:
*"Six... seven... eight... nine... ten. Or if that was a mic check rather than my cue, say so and
I'll stop."*

Three things about that are each worth stopping a publish for, and they compound:

1. **It puts words in his mouth, in his own thread, permanently.** A user bubble reading
   *"1, 2, 3, 4, 5."* sits in his conversation. He did not say it. It is still there after a quit
   and a relaunch (`a5-11`, `a5-12`).
2. **It spends a model turn on his subscription** that he did not ask for. Mine cost him one today.
3. **It can chain.** The answer to the self-prompt is itself spoken aloud, into the same
   microphone, in the same conditions. Nothing I saw stops the second lap; what stopped mine was
   me turning voice off.

And the inversion is exact, in the same eight seconds: **the app discarded the human and accepted
itself.** My deliberate talk-over (*"Excuse me Rich, you can stop counting now."*, spoken at
20:06:43.3Z) was discarded as audio it could not vouch for — and Rich's own voice, in the same
window, was not.

**Your blocker #1 is fixed in the way you asked and is not fixed in effect.** The accusatory
sentence is gone; the new sentence is conditional, honest, and appears once per voice session, as
built. But it still fires on Rich's own echo while the user is silent — proven again here with the
muted control — so the brief's own acceptance criterion for this walk ("no notice must appear")
is not met. On its own I would have called that Medium-High and argued about it. It is not on its
own.

**What is genuinely fixed, and it is not a short list:** the canceller no longer claims confidence
it does not have (it now reports *"cannot vouch"* with the measured gap, erle −2.9 dB and −2.2 dB
on this rig); the notice copy is the honest one and latches once per voice session; the ticker
orphan is gone across 20 observations; the bottom of Settings is reachable at 1024x700 and
**"Bust a bug!"** can be clicked from the window the app restores itself; a typed turn is the same
shape and the same speed as candidate .4 through the new work-lease path; the quit is clean and the
thread survives it.

**Basis: ran it on screen; log and ledger as the counterpart.**

One playback: `say "Please count slowly out loud from one to fifteen."`, which ended at 20:02:56Z.

**No confident-while-removing-nothing anywhere in this walk.** Every tainted discard printed the
measured gap and said it could not vouch. The fix the engineer describes holds on this rig.

### Observation C — one deliberate talk-over, and the defect that came out of it

**Basis: ran it on screen; the ledger with millisecond timestamps.**

Volume back to 85, same voice session. One spoken prompt (*"Please count slowly out loud from one
to twenty."*), then **one** `say` over Rich's answer, 3 s after his deltas started flowing:
*"Excuse me Rich, you can stop counting now."*

What the brief asked me to check, answered first:

- **Rich was not cut off.** He finished, and the second turn finished too.
- **No second notice appeared.** The once-per-voice-session latch held, in the session where the
  notice had already fired. (*Basis: read from source* for the mechanism —
  `richos/app/crates/richos-voice/src/controller.rs:1452`, `half_duplex.discard_during_playout()`
  — and **ran it on screen** for the behavior.)
- **My talk-over never reached him**, and the log says why: a second tainted discard, erle −2.2 dB.

**It is not my harness speech, and the timestamps settle it.** My two playbacks that minute were
*"Please count slowly out loud from one to twenty"* (already recorded at 20:06:35.672) and
*"Excuse me Rich, you can stop counting now"*, which began at 20:06:43.3 — 0.4 s before this
prompt landed, and recognition of a four-second phrase cannot finish in 0.4 s. The text itself is
the tell: *"1, 2, 3, 4, 5."* is Rich's own counting, and nothing else in the room said it.

**For the engineer, what this walk can and cannot show.** *Basis: read from the running app's
state/log.* The sequence above is the whole of what was recorded: the ledger carries no playout
boundaries and no per-utterance `Started`/`tainted` flag, and `app.log` carries no timestamps, so

after playout had been marked ended.** What the record does establish: the model turn ended at
20:06:40.680; the app itself reports that **Rich was still speaking** after that, because the
discard it printed in this window is the one that says *"discarded audio captured while Rich was
speaking"* and it applied to my talk-over at 20:06:43.3; **two** tainted discards were printed in
this window and the admitted utterance was not one of them. (I have no ears — every statement
about what was audible comes from the app's own log, never from me.) **What would settle the cause:** a
log line at playout start and end with the wall clock, and the per-utterance `tainted` flag printed
whether true or false — neither exists today, and both are in the same file that already prints
the ERLE gap.
