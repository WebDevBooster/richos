including paths whose final directories do not exist yet. It also refuses

stops if recording its integration branch fails. Previously it ignored that
failure, making the guard abstain and invalidating mutation evidence.

## Full engine verification

The initial full run completed all 141 CI units covering 118 discovered suites
in four isolated clones at `db92ef8a`. The receipt inventory has exactly the
141 expected unit IDs, with no omissions or duplicates. It recorded 132 passes,
six failures and three timeouts. The original result remains a failed run.

Subsequent fixes and reruns provide passing evidence for **all 141 units**.
The coverage check matched every original unit ID to a successful original
result or a successful rerun. No engine unit remains unresolved.

Both long workspace retries passed at `9885955f`:

| Workspace manager | 68 behavior tests and all 56 mutations passed | 876.1 seconds |

Their receipts passed the official verifier. The implementation, both suites
and their mutation campaigns have identical Git blobs at the original run,
retry and final code revisions.

[Committed evidence](repo-audit-recheck-2026-09-15/README.md) retains the original
failed receipts separately from passing reruns. Full local logs remain under
`/tmp/richos-recheck-20260915` and `/tmp/richos-recheck-*.log`.

Failures and their resolutions:

- The demo cleanup test saw a concurrent smoke-demo directory because both
  launches shared the host temporary directory. Its retry with a dedicated
  `TMPDIR` passed. Original failure retained.
- The claim-gate contract section exposed the additional relocation defect above.
  Both mutations that misfired are now caught by the corrected fixture.
- The census suite omitted checks registered through the dispatcher. The fixed
  test independently expands the manifest and verifies identities.
- The by-reference suite reached its deadline under parallel load. Two pointer
  checks also inherited the launcher's `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`, while their fixtures
  redirected only `HOME`. Both probes now set their sandbox config directory.
  Its full retry passed all 53 checks with an unrelated ambient override.
- The dispatcher suite depended on an ignored generated manifest checksum and
  the caller's working directory. Its fixture now exercises installation in a
  disposable copy and checks the filesystem fallback from a neutral directory.
- The dialect suite still inferred repository and skill locations from the old
  engine depth. It now resolves the Git root and uses the actual engine root.
- The global-state witness inventory omitted the dispatcher fixture. Its scanner
  originally matched a diagnostic string; after the dispatcher fixture was
  fixed, the installer invocation is real and sandboxed. The inventory now names
  the fixture and its protection. All 13 checks passed.
- Both workspace suites passed their behavior checks but exceeded their unit
  deadlines during mutation campaigns under parallel load. Sequential retries
  with four mutation workers and a 7,200-second per-unit limit passed. No shipped
  CI deadline or test weight was changed to obtain these results.

The affected-unit runner passed all three units for the claim and census
changes at `5712d56b`, including the claim contract section. Its receipt verifier
confirmed complete coverage and a single revision for that group. A further
three dependency units passed at `ba709363`. The final installer witness change
and its two dependencies passed at `fb94f088`. Both groups passed the official
receipt verifier. An earlier claim/census attempt was invalidated by a canary because it was launched from a working
checkout that was still being edited; the isolated rerun passed.
