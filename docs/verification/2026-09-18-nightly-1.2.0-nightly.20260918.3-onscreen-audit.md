## 2. Barge-in — ONE measurement, the CEO at the desk

**This is the question the build exists to answer, and the answer is yes.**

### The evidence that counts, first

The CEO was at the desk, spoke over Rich in his own voice, and gave his verdict in his own words:

> **"my barge in worked fine this time"**

That is the finding. §53 asks for a human at the microphone, and this is that human saying what
happened to him. Everything below corroborates it and does not outrank it.

### How the walk was run

I typed the request (voice mode removes the composer entirely — see the note at the end of this
section), then turned voice on so the answer would be spoken:

> Please count slowly out loud from one to thirty. Just say the numbers, nothing else.

Rich began speaking the count. The CEO spoke over him. Here is the build's own record of that one
session (`13:17:42Z`–`13:19:47Z`), unedited:

              · barge-in=15 of 25 frames (0.240 s of near-end speech inside 0.400 s), gated on the
                canceller's per-frame near-end verdict from the first frame
13:17:45.273Z ANSWER AUDIBLE — half-duplex window OPEN
13:17:45.561Z playout chunk START — 0.421 s queued, 13 sentence(s) still in synthesis
13:17:49.880Z utterance START tainted=true heard-rich=true (Rich audible=true)

**In plain terms:**

- Rich started speaking at `13:17:45.561`.
- The CEO's voice was recognized as near-end speech at `13:17:49.880` — **4.319 s into the count**.
- Rich's mouth stopped at `13:17:50.019` — **139 ms after the onset**, with 4.751 s of speech
  already synthesized and queued thrown straight in the bin, and 13 more sentences abandoned in
  synthesis.
- The CEO's full **7.344 s** sentence was **ADMITTED**, not discarded. Rich heard all of it.
- Rich answered *that* sentence 4.8 s later.

**What he actually said and what came back** (`14-voice-t04.png`, `14-voice-t12.png`):

> **CEO:** You know what? I've changed my mind. I want you to tell me what's the capital of Germany.
> **Rich:** Berlin.
> **CEO:** Perfect. And what's the capital of Poland?
> **Rich:** Warsaw.

Two turns, both spoken over or after the cut, both heard, both answered in one word. The
candidate-.7 complaint — *"no matter what I said, he couldn't hear me"*, *"He couldn't hear
anything at all"* — **does not reproduce on .9**, and the man who made that complaint says so
himself.

**At which number was he cut?** I will not guess. 4.319 s of playout at "Daniel" 180 wpm lands
somewhere in the low teens, but the log does not record the spoken word and I was not in the room
to hear it. The on-screen transcript shows the full count One…Thirty (`14-voice-t02.png`) because
only the *playout* was cut, not the text. **Unmeasured, and it does not change the verdict.**

### The negative control came free, in the same session

**That is the right behavior for the failure case.** It does not silently swallow him; it tells him

speaking — the only channel is the one that was just failing on .7. It did not block this walk
(I sequenced around it), and it may well be deliberate. I am recording it because it is the reason
a typed request had to precede the voice turn, and because "type it instead" is the natural
recovery a person reaches for when speech is not landing.
