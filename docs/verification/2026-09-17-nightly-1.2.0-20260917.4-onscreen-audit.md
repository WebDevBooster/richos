## Verdict

**NOT READY.**

One defect blocks it, and it is in the feature this candidate was built to fix.

**On speakers, Rich tells the CEO he interrupted when the CEO said nothing.** The new
talked-over notice fires on Rich's own voice coming back through the microphone. I proved it:
a spoken turn at output volume 85 produced the notice while I was provably silent, and the
*same* spoken turn repeated with the output silenced produced no notice and no discard at all.
The CEO's setup — Mac mini speakers, an Elgato Wave:3 on the desk — is exactly that path, and
he drove the talk control himself during the last walk. So on his machine every spoken answer
ends with Rich saying, in the thread, under Rich's own byline:

> *"You started talking while I was still speaking, and I couldn't separate your voice from
> mine — so that didn't reach me and I haven't sent anything. I'm listening now."*

Nothing of his was lost. The sentence is not merely noise; it is **false**, and it is false
about something he did. Audit-3's version of this defect was a *silent* limitation — this
candidate converts silence into a wrong accusation, on every spoken answer, and calls it a fix.
That is worse for a person than what it replaced, and it is the first thing he will meet when he
tests the nightly with voice.

**And one of the six fixes is not in effect on screen.** Fix #9 (the home screen's temporary
line no longer leaves one word alone) does not work in the shipped build: two of the four ticker
lines I observed still break with the last word alone on line two, including the exact sentence
the commit quotes.

Everything else in this candidate is better than candidate .2, in places substantially so, and
none of the remaining findings blocks a person from using it.

## 3. Audit-3 #1 and #4, on screen

### #4 — a discarded utterance now says so, on the first occurrence: **FIXED**

**Basis: ran it on screen (`a4-16`), with the log as the counterpart.**

Voice on and listening, I played a 1.1 s non-speech sound (a synthesized two-burst throat-clear,
`cough.wav`, generated by the harness and played with `afplay` — named because it is not a human
noise). **The first** occurrence produced, in the thread:

> *"That didn't come through as speech, so I haven't sent anything. I'm still listening."*

The log's counterpart:

```
[richos-voice] refused before recognition — the audio carried no voice: windows=108 voiced=0
   run=0 (0.032 s of 0.112 s needed) f0=0.0 Hz +/-0.0 Hz (0.00 % of 2.00 % needed)
   level -70.1..-27.1 dBFS
```

That is the pre-whisper gate, so the line shown is `HeardNoVoice` rather than `DidNotCatchThat`.
Both are the same event to a person — *you made a sound, nothing was sent* — and the thing
audit-3 asked for is there: **it speaks the first time, and it says what did not happen.** No
model turn was spent. I did not separately reach the post-whisper `(clears throat)` path.

### #1 — being talked over is no longer silent, but the notice is wrong on speakers: **BLOCKER**

**Basis: ran it on screen, twice, with a control.**

**Observation A (`a4-14`, `a4-15`).** Volume 85. I spoke *"Please count slowly out loud from one
to fifteen."* — captured and transcribed correctly. Rich answered **completely**: *"One... two...
three... … fifteen."*, *Worked for 6s*. **Rich's answer was not cut off**, which is the one thing
the brief asked me to check about the notice, and it passes. Then the notice appeared:

> *"You started talking while I was still speaking, and I couldn't separate your voice from mine
> — so that didn't reach me and I haven't sent anything. I'm listening now."*

**I said nothing.** Between my utterance ending (15:44:40Z, `say` is blocking and had returned)
and the notice, my process ran `sleep` and nothing else. The log:

```
[richos-voice] discarded audio captured while Rich was speaking — the echo canceller has not
   proven itself yet (erle=0 dB, needs 2.000 s of Rich speaking then 2.000 s held below the
   leak floor); barge-in still needs 5.008 s of talking over him until it does
```

**Observation B — the control (`a4-17`).** I repeated the identical spoken turn, and the instant
`say` returned (15:47:36.338Z) I set the output volume to 0 (15:47:36.430Z), so Rich's answer was
spoken into a silent room. Everything else was the same: same me, same silence, same microphone,
same sentence, same 5 s answer.

```
prompt received 15:47:37Z  source=jam   "Please count slowly out loud from 1 to 15 again."
TurnCompleted  15:47:42Z   end_turn
app.log since: (nothing)
```

**No discard. No notice.** The single variable between A and B is whether Rich's voice was
audible in the room.

**So the notice is triggered by Rich's own voice returning through the microphone.** It is not a
timing coincidence and it is not my speech. The consequence for a person on speakers is that
every spoken answer ends with Rich telling him he interrupted — and the sentence's own load-bearing
clause, *"that didn't reach me and I haven't sent anything"*, is false, because he sent nothing to
lose.

**Mitigation, stated fairly:** the voice bar carries the standing hint *"headphones recommended ·
tap ⏺ to end voice"*, and on headphones this cannot happen. That is a real mitigation and it is
why I ranked the underlying echo problem as a design constraint rather than a crash. It is not
enough to publish on, because the app does not *require* headphones, does not detect their
absence, and the thing it says when they are absent is not true.

**Not verified:** whether a genuine human talk-over produces the same notice. It should, by the
code path — the same `talked_over_discard()` latch serves both — but I have no voice to prove it,
and one person speaking over Rich once would settle it.

## 4. The other things a person would hit

**Defect 3 — the bottom of Settings is unreachable at the size the app restores itself.**
*Basis: ran it on screen, with a positive control.* The window is 1024x700 at (664,108) — the

**Defect 4 — the corpus sheet returns at every launch.** *Basis: ran it on screen, across a

answered three times.

**Defect 5 — `CAPITAL`'s count is unreadable.** *Basis: ran it on screen, stable across two

`LEGAL & RISK` caption; the digits' lower half is cut (`a4-01c`). It is a number on the screen

**Defect 6 — the update block opens red, and misdescribes why.** *Basis: ran it on screen; ground
truth from the endpoint.* Settings shows, in red: *"The update server answered, but not with

**Defect 7 — the entity picker is a dead end for a screen reader, and has no visible way out.**

**Defect 8 — a notice looks like an answer.** *Basis: ran it on screen (`a4-15`).* The voice
