## 2. The blocker, on speakers, with the muted control

### Observation A — silent through Rich's answer, at volume 85

**Basis: ran it on screen; log and ledger as the counterpart.**

Voice on (`a6-07`). One playback: `say "Please count slowly out loud from one to twenty."`, ended
21:31:09Z. **I was silent from that moment on** — my process ran `sleep` and nothing else.

Recognized in full and sent. Rich answered with the whole count to twenty and **was not cut off**.
The model turn ended at `21:31:30.835Z`.

**This is the window that broke candidate .5**, and this is what the app did in it:

```
21:31:28.632Z ANSWER AUDIBLE — half-duplex window OPEN (hold after the last sample 32 ms …)
21:31:29.443Z playout chunk START — 0.421 s queued, 17 sentence(s) still in synthesis
21:31:29.694Z utterance START tainted=true (Rich audible=true)
21:31:29.886Z playout chunk END — queue empty, 17 sentence(s) still in synthesis   <- a GAP
21:31:30.503Z utterance END DISCARDED tainted=true (Rich audible=true)
21:31:30.835Z (the MODEL turn ends here — the speaking does not)
   … 16 more chunk END/START gaps, the window still OPEN through every one …
21:31:45.124Z answer over — half-duplex window CLOSED, the microphone is the CEO's again
```

- The window opened **2.2 s before** the model turn ended and closed **14.3 s after** it.
- **Eighteen** chunk-END-to-chunk-START gaps sat inside it, each one "queue empty, N sentence(s)
  still in synthesis" — precisely the state the old rule scored as silence.
- Three utterances were born in there. All three printed `tainted=true` at **start and end**, and
  all three were discarded.
- **Nothing was submitted, and no model turn was spent.**

**But this is also where defect 1 lives.** When the answer finished, three notice cards were
stacked in his thread (`a6-08`), for an answer he had said nothing during:

> *"While I was speaking, I couldn't tell your voice from my own — so if you said something just
> then, it didn't reach me and I haven't sent anything. I'm listening now."*
>
> *"That didn't come through as speech, so I haven't sent anything. I'm still listening."*
>
> *"I didn't catch that, so I haven't sent anything. I'm still listening."*

Three apologies for nothing, to a man who was silent. **Basis for the mechanism: read from
source** — `richos/app/crates/richos-voice/src/controller.rs` carries three separate latches,
`CouldNotListenWhileSpeaking` "latched once per voice session" (`:1749`), `HeardNoVoice` and
`DidNotCatchThat` each "latched per RUN" (`:1139`, `:1141`). Each one individually honors "at most
once". Together they cost him three cards. The brief's expectation — *"On speakers you should see
it at most once per voice session"* — **is literally true of that one notice and not true of what
a person sees.**

### Observation B — the control: the same turn with the output silenced

**Basis: ran it on screen.**

Identical spoken prompt; the instant `say` returned (21:33:32Z) I set output volume to 0, so Rich's

21:33:47.491Z ANSWER AUDIBLE — half-duplex window OPEN

**Zero utterances. Zero discards. Zero notice cards** (`a6-09`). The single variable between A and

### Observation C — one deliberate talk-over

**Basis: ran it on screen; the ledger with millisecond timestamps.**

Volume back to 85, same voice session. One spoken prompt, then **one** `say` over Rich's answer at
`21:35:01.262Z`, 11 s into his counting: *"Excuse me Rich, you can stop counting now."*

What the brief asked, answered first:

- **Rich was not cut off.** He finished the count to twenty; the chunk log runs down to
  "0 sentence(s) still in synthesis" (`a6-11`).
- **No second notice appeared** — none at all, in fact (`a6-11`).
- **Nothing of Rich's was submitted.** The ledger's last entry for that turn is `TurnCompleted`.

And then the half of this that matters more than the half I was sent to check:

**My talk-over produced no utterance at all — and he was told nothing.** There is no
`utterance START` anywhere near `21:35:01`; the endpointer never registered an onset while Rich was
mid-chunk. So the one moment in this walk when a person genuinely spoke and was genuinely not
heard is the moment the app said **nothing**, because all three notice latches had already been
spent in observation A on Rich's own echo.

**That is defect 1 stated properly:** the notices fire three times when he is silent and zero times
when he is not heard. It is exactly backwards from what serves him.

### Does any of this stay in his thread?

**No, and this is the finding that decides the severity.** *Basis: ran it on screen.* After the
quit and relaunch I scrolled back to the exact region between observation A's answer and
observation B's prompt: **the three notice cards are gone** (`a6-18`). They are transient session
UI. Candidate .5's echo-born user bubble, by contrast, is still sitting in the thread where it was
(`a6-17`) — permanent, because it was a real message.

So defect 1 is noise he reads once per voice session and never sees again. Defect .5's blocker was
a sentence he never said, in his thread, forever. That is the difference between NOT READY and
READY.

**Basis: read from the running app's state/log.** Every turn I ran was slower than the matching
candidate-.5 turn — five out of five, 1.8x to 2.7x. That looked like a regression, and it is not
