Connection and settings

Your Mac remains the home of your conversations. This phone stores a recent view and unsent work.

Moving from the PWA? First send or copy its pending messages. Pairing this app replaces that phone’s access; it does not import its keys or unsent work.

Reply notifications use RichOS’s hosted service and Apple, including when you connect through Tailscale. Alerts contain no conversation text.

Talk to Rich from your phone

Open phone pairing on your Mac and choose RichOS Connect or Tailscale. If you choose Tailscale, keep it connected when away from home.
