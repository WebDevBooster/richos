Why this is needed at all

The probe has to be served over HTTPS. An iPhone gives a plain page no microphone, no notifications and no Add to Home Screen, so without HTTPS there is nothing to test.

HTTPS needs a certificate, and a certificate is normally bought from a company on the internet that has never seen your Mac. Your Mac made its own instead, a minute ago, and no company was involved. That is the whole reason your phone needs telling: nobody it already trusts has vouched for this one.

What it covers, and this is the entire list: {{CERT_NAMES}}. It cannot be used for anything on the internet, and the key that signs it never leaves your Mac — it is in one file, readable only by you: {{CA_PATH}}

!

Two things, before you start

iOS will show the words “Not Signed” in red. That is not a fault and it cannot be fixed. Getting rid of that red text means buying a certificate from Apple to sign the file with, and we are not putting one inside a free app to quiet a warning. The red means nobody vouched for this file except your own Mac — which is the entire point of it.

If step 3's switch is not there at all, stop and tell Rich. Some iOS versions have a bug where a certificate you installed by hand never appears under Certificate Trust Settings. That is Apple's bug, not yours and not a mistake you made — and it is one of the most useful things this whole probe could discover, because it would mean this design cannot work on your phone. Do not hunt for it for more than a minute.

3

Switch it on the step everyone misses

Installing it is not enough. Apple requires one more switch, or the certificate sits there doing nothing and the probe page still will not open. Still in Settings:

  1. General
  2. About
  3. scroll to the bottom: Certificate Trust Settings
  4. turn on the switch beside {{CA_NAME}}
  5. Continue

Your Mac calls this certificate by six words. They are the same six words the phone app will show you when you pair it, and they are how you tell your Mac's certificate from anybody else's:

{{CA_WORDS}}

The same thing as digits, if you want to compare it character by character

SHA-256 {{CA_FINGERPRINT}}

To undo all of this, in one move: Settings, General, VPN & Device Management, tap {{CA_NAME}}, Remove Profile. Your phone is then exactly as it was. Nothing was sent anywhere, no account was made, and this page has no analytics and no JavaScript at all.
