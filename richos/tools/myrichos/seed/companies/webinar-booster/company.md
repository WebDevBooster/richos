# Webinar Booster

Written 2026-09-06 from the sources named at the bottom. **Not yet confirmed by him.**

## What it is

Webinar Booster runs automated webinars that feel live.

A viewer registers through a form on the client's own funnel and is given a start time that is
close at hand rather than days away. At that time a pre-recorded webinar plays as though it were
happening now: no pause, no skipping ahead, chat messages appearing alongside, and offers
appearing at the moments they were scheduled for. Everything the viewer does flows straight back
into the client's CRM.

It serves several clients at once, each on their own web address.

## Who it is for

Businesses that sell through webinars and want the results of running one every day without
presenting one every day. They already run their funnels and their contact database in
GoHighLevel, and this has to fit into that rather than replace it.

## What it has to get right

- **The illusion has to hold.** A viewer who can pause, skip or rewind knows immediately that it
  is not live, and the whole premise goes with it.
- **The registration and the follow-up belong to the client**, in the client's own CRM, under the
  client's own domain. This runs the room; it does not own the relationship.
- **Every client is separated from every other.** One client's viewers, recordings and results
  are never visible to another.

## Words used here

- **Just in time** — offering a start time that is minutes away rather than days.
- **Session** — one viewer's run through one webinar.
- **Simulated chat** — the messages shown alongside the video.
- **CTA** — an offer shown at a scheduled moment during the webinar.
