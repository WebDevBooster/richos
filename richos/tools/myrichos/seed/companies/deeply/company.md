# Deeply

Written 2026-09-06 from the sources named at the bottom. **Not yet confirmed by him.**

## What it is

Deeply is **a game for discovering extraordinary people** — deliberately the opposite of Tinder.

Where the mainstream apps sort people by appearance and reward swiping, Deeply is built so that
people meet through real conversation, and it makes that conversation fun rather than worthy.
The line it rests on: **deep connections start with deep conversations.**

It is marketed as the most enjoyable way to find people you genuinely click with.

## The bigger idea, which is not the pitch

Internally the ambition is larger than dating — an engine for meaningful conversation that would
work just as well for finding business partners, travel companions or friends. **That is
deliberately not how it is marketed at launch.** Anyone talking about it publicly talks about
dating.

## What it has to get right

- **A balanced room.** The product is designed around an even split between men and women, and
  the design treats the two sides differently on purpose, because their experience of dating apps
  is not the same.
- **Conversation over appearance.** Any feature that makes the product faster to skim makes it
  worse. That is the whole premise, not a preference.

## House rules

Two are enforced on every design brief before work starts:

- **The settings button is locked.** One exact component, on every page and every screen, with no
  exceptions.
- **16 pixels is the floor for text that matters.** Important text that is not a headline and not
  uppercase is at least 16px. Smaller type tells a reader the words are skippable, and this is a
  product where the words are the point. Founder ruling, 2026-07-22.

## Words used here

- **The game** — the product. It is described as a game rather than an app, on purpose.
- **Booster** — a prompt that takes a conversation deeper.
- **Extraordinary people** — the marketing phrase for who a member is trying to find.
